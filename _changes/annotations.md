title: Add notes to your traffic chart with annotations
date: 2026-07-14
---
You can now add annotations to your traffic chart to mark important events (like product launches, marketing campaigns, SEO changes, etc.) that help explain a spike or drop in your stats.

You can add personal notes visible only to you, or site notes that are visible to everyone with access to the dashboard.

[Learn more in our annotations docs](https://plausible.io/docs/annotations).
