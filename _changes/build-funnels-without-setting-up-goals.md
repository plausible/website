---
layout: change
title: Build funnels without setting up goals first
date: 2026-11-02
---
You no longer need to set up goals before building a funnel.

When adding a step, start typing to find pages visitors have viewed or custom events they've triggered on your site. Select a page or event to add it to your funnel. You can still use your existing goals as steps too.

[Learn more in our funnel analysis docs](https://plausible.io/docs/funnel-analysis).
