title: Flexible funnels
date: 2026-09-23
---
You can now choose **Flexible** funnels as a funnel type, where visitors only need to complete the first and last steps. Any steps in between are optional.

For example, in a "View product → View details → Purchase" funnel, visitors who skip "View details" still count as converted. You can see how many visitors make a purchase, even if they take different paths to get there.

Choose **Flexible** when adding or editing a funnel in your site settings.
