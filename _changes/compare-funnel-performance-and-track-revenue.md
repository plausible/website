layout: change
title: Compare funnels over time and track revenue in funnels
date: 2026-09-09T01:47:32.000Z
---
You can now compare [funnel performance](https://plausible.io/docs/funnel-analysis) across time periods. Select "Compare" from the date range picker (or press X) to see conversion rates for both periods side by side at every step, with arrows highlighting increases and declines.

Funnels that include any [revenue goals](https://plausible.io/docs/ecommerce-revenue-tracking) now also show total revenue and revenue per visitor at the relevant step. This shows you where visitors convert or drop off and how much revenue those conversions bring in.
