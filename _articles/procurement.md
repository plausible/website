Plausible is a simple, privacy-first alternative to Google Analytics. Buying it is self-service too. You can [create an account](https://plausible.io/register), start a free trial and purchase a subscription online. This page brings together the information you need for your internal purchasing process.

## Getting started

You can evaluate Plausible with a free trial. No credit card is required. When you're ready to subscribe, choose a plan and complete payment through your account.

See our [pricing](https://plausible.io/#pricing) for available plans. Our docs explain [how to subscribe](https://plausible.io/docs/trial-to-paid), [payment methods and billing](https://plausible.io/docs/billing) and [how to view and save invoices](https://plausible.io/docs/download-invoices).

## How payment works

Subscriptions are paid by card, PayPal, Apple Pay or Google Pay at checkout and renew automatically. Paddle automatically emails you an invoice after every payment. Paddle applies any sales tax or VAT. You can add your VAT number during checkout.

If your organization cannot purchase through checkout, you can add a reseller or procurement partner to your team with the [Billing role](https://plausible.io/docs/users-roles#inviting-team-members-and-assigning-roles), so they handle payment while you keep control of the team.

## Who provides the service and who receives payment?

Plausible Analytics is developed and operated by **Plausible Insights OÜ**, a company registered in Estonia.

Subscriptions are sold through **Paddle**, our authorized reseller and merchant of record. Paddle handles the purchasing transaction, invoicing, payments and applicable sales taxes. Plausible provides the analytics service and product support.

For your internal records:

| Information needed | Entity to use |
|---|---|
| Software provider, product support and service security | Plausible Insights OÜ |
| Invoice issuer, payment recipient and payee tax documentation | The Paddle entity shown on your invoice or checkout |

Paddle currently lists the following entities by transaction location. Always use the entity shown on your invoice or checkout for your vendor records and tax documentation:

| Your location | Paddle entity |
|---|---|
| United States | Paddle.com Inc., a Delaware corporation |
| Everywhere else | Paddle.com Market Ltd., United Kingdom |

See Paddle's [current vendor information](https://www.paddle.com/about/vendor-information) for company and tax details. Its [procurement guidance](https://www.paddle.com/about/procurement) covers purchasing terms and how to request bank confirmation documents.

## Tax documents

For purchases billed by Paddle, payee tax documentation should correspond to the Paddle entity on the transaction.

We can provide Paddle's W-9 form on request. [Email us](mailto:hello@plausible.io) if you need a copy.

Paddle links to certificates of residence on its [procurement page](https://www.paddle.com/about/procurement).
